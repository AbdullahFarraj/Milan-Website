HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering materials, delivery, and payments.

Do you deliver materials directly to construction and fit-out sites?

Yes, we provide same-day and scheduled site deliveries across all Dubai districts (including Al Qusais, Deira, Business Bay, Downtown, Dubai Hills, and JLT) as well as Sharjah, Ajman, and all UAE Emirates with our dedicated fleet.

Do you offer special contractor discounts and bulk trade pricing?

Absolutely. We work closely with fit-out companies, MEP contractors, and maintenance teams. We provide tiered volume discounts, project-specific quotations, and official VAT tax invoices for all transactions.

Can you provide computerized paint color mixing for Jotun and Berger?

Yes! We operate advanced in-store computerized paint tinting systems capable of matching any RAL, NCS, or custom designer color code on the spot in matte, silk, or gloss finishes.

Can an outdoor sales representative visit our project site?

Yes, our Outdoor Sales team (managed by Mr. Printo - 055 365 6298) is available to visit your site anywhere in Dubai for material estimation, sample viewing, and technical advice.

What are your showroom hours and exact location?

Our showroom is open 7 days a week, Monday through Sunday from 7:30 AM to 8:30 PM. We are located at Ali Saeed Abdullah Bel Hab Al Ameri Bldg., Shop No-11, Al Qusais Ind First, Dubai, with easy truck loading access.
